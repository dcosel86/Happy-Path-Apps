⭐ How it plays

A few minutes a day. Lots of little wins.

Every day there’s a fresh plan of games picked for your child’s age and goals. Short rounds keep it fun, and there are no timers or scores to worry about.

❓ Questions

Good questions, grown-ups.

What ages is Omilía Kids for?

Children ages 3 and up. The youngest games are built for 3 to 5 year olds, and the app picks games based on your child’s age and what you’d like to work on.

Does my child need to be able to read?

Nope! Round prompts are read aloud, so children who can’t read yet can play on their own.

Are there ads?

No, never. There are no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking. Your child’s name stays on your device, and we don’t sell or share your child’s information.

Is Omilía Kids speech therapy?

Omilía Kids is a practice tool. It isn’t a substitute for an assessment or therapy with a licensed speech-language pathologist, but it’s a fun way to keep practicing at home or between sessions.

How much does it cost?

Omilía Kids starts with a free 7-day trial, then a monthly or yearly subscription. You can cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes — Omilía Kids works on both iPhone and iPad.
