The Story of Omilía

I had never heard of aphasia. That is, until January of 2020 when my mother-in-law suffered a severe stroke that left her needing to learn how to communicate all over again — a devastating condition that disrupted her every day life. But it’s not just her. Millions of individuals around the world suffer from aphasia and other similar disorders related to traumatic brain injuries.

I wanted to help. I noticed a gap in existing therapy apps and thought I could use my skills as a software engineer to create a better tool to aid in her recovery. Omilía was born.

Since then, the app has grown and evolved into what it is today — including dozens of unique and fun exercises, tasks and challenges. More recently it has expanded to include a Therapist app in an effort to tighten the patient-therapist feedback loop.

Ultimately, the purpose of Omilía is to support individuals facing a difficult season of life and to offer hope. If one person is encouraged as a result of this app, it’s all worth it.
