Why Coach Assist

Coaching youth sports is a juggling act. Clock, score, matchups, and a bench full of kids who all want to play. Coach Assist keeps track of the minutes so you can focus on coaching.

How it works

From tip-off to final whistle.

1

Set up the game

Pick your roster, quarters or halves, and period length. Sensible youth defaults for every sport.

2

Track it live

Run the clock and keep score. Every player’s time on court is counted automatically.

3

Sub with confidence

Smart suggestions pair who’s played the most with who’s waited the longest. Apply one or all with a tap.

4

Share the report

A clean playing time report for every game, ready to share with parents in seconds.

Everything on the sideline
