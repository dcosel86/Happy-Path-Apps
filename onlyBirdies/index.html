Why OnlyBirdies

No more scorecards on napkins or doing Nassau math in the cart. OnlyBirdies keeps score for the whole group and tracks every bet, so you can focus on the round.

Scoring

Keep score easily.

A scorecard that stays out of your way, with stats and achievements that make every round worth remembering.

  • Gross or net — handicaps applied hole by hole
  • Stroke Play, Match Play, Stableford — pick your format
  • Stats that matter — scoring breakdowns, par 3/4/5 averages, streaks
  • Achievements — celebrate your first birdie and personal bests
Side games

No napkins. Just golf.

Bring the whole group — even across multiple tee times — and let OnlyBirdies handle the money.

  • Every bet tracked automatically as scores go in
  • Live leaderboard across every group on the course
  • Settle up in one tap — see who owes whom and pay with Venmo
